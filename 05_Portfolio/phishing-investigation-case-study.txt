# Phishing Email Investigation
## SOC Analyst Portfolio Case Study

### Project Summary
I completed a simulated phishing email investigation to practice identifying suspicious email characteristics, extracting indicators, reviewing security-tool output, and documenting evidence.

### Scenario
A fictional email claimed that a Microsoft 365 account would be disabled unless the recipient completed an account-verification process.

### Investigation Activities
1. Reviewed the email subject, sender, recipient, and URL.
2. Identified urgency, an account-suspension threat, and a verification request as potential phishing indicators.
3. Used CyberChef to extract the URL and email addresses from the sample.
4. Submitted a manually constructed practice header to Google Admin Toolbox Messageheader and documented the displayed SPF, DKIM, and DMARC failures.
5. Queried `example.com` in VirusTotal and recorded the observed detection ratio of 0/92.
6. Used Windows PowerShell to calculate a SHA-256 hash of the sample email.
7. Documented the evidence, indicators, limitations, and recommended actions.

### Tools
- CyberChef
- Google Admin Toolbox Messageheader
- VirusTotal
- Windows PowerShell
- Notepad
- File Explorer

### Key Learning Outcomes
- Recognizing common phishing characteristics
- Extracting URLs and email addresses
- Understanding basic email authentication checks
- Interpreting URL reputation results cautiously
- Calculating SHA-256 file hashes
- Maintaining evidence records and communicating findings

### Conclusion
The exercise demonstrates a structured approach to phishing triage and investigation documentation. The sample email and practice header were fictional. No real malicious URL, compromised account, or phishing incident was confirmed.
