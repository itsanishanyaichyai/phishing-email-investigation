# VirusTotal URL Analysis

**Project:** Phishing Email Investigation  
**Tool:** VirusTotal  
**URL analyzed:** `https://example.com`  
**Detection result:** 0/92

## Findings
At the time of analysis, VirusTotal reported that 0 of 92 security vendors flagged the example domain as malicious.

## Interpretation
No detections were reported in this result. However, a zero-detection result does not guarantee that a URL is safe. Additional evidence and context are necessary to determine whether a URL is associated with phishing.

## Limitation
The domain `example.com` is used for practice and documentation. This result is a baseline exercise, not evidence of a real phishing incident.

## Analyst conclusion
I practiced reviewing URL reputation results and documenting detection ratios, findings, and limitations using VirusTotal.
