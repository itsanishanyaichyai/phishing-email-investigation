PHISHING INDICATORS — CASE PHISH-001

Indicator 1: Urgent subject line
Evidence: "URGENT: Your Microsoft 365 Account Will Be Disabled"
Analysis: The subject creates pressure to make the recipient act quickly.

Indicator 2: Threat of account suspension
Evidence: "Your account will be permanently disabled within 24 hours."
Analysis: The threat may cause fear and discourage the recipient from verifying the message.

Indicator 3: Unverified sender
Evidence: "IT Support"
Analysis: The display name claims to represent IT Support, but the sender's identity has not been verified.

Indicator 4: Generic greeting
Evidence: "Dear User"
Analysis: A generic greeting can appear in mass-distributed emails, but it does not prove phishing by itself.

Indicator 5: Account verification link
Evidence: The email asks the recipient to verify their account through a link.
Analysis: A fraudulent verification page could be used to steal credentials, but this sample does not establish that credential theft occurred.

Indicator 6: Suspicious-looking URL
Evidence: microsoft365-security-check.example.invalid
Analysis: The URL contains Microsoft-related wording, but the reserved .invalid domain indicates that this is a fictional training example.

Conclusion:
The email contains several phishing indicators. Because it is fictional, no real attacker or malicious campaign has been confirmed.
