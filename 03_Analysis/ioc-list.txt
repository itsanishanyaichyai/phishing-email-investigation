## 2. Email Authentication Evidence

**Evidence ID:** E-005  
**Tool:** Google Admin Toolbox Messageheader

The manually constructed practice header displayed SPF, DKIM, and DMARC failures. These results are simulated and do not verify the authentication status of a real email.

## 3. Threat Intelligence Evidence

**Tool:** VirusTotal  
**Practice domain:** example.com  
**Observed detection ratio:** 0/92

This result applies only to the practice domain. It does not establish the reputation or safety of the fictional URL in the sample email.

## 4. Limitations

- All email addresses and the phishing URL are fictional.
- No real malicious domain or IP address has been confirmed.
- No genuine email authentication results have been verified.
- Extracting an indicator does not prove that it is malicious.

## 5. Analyst Conclusion

This register documents indicators extracted from a simulated phishing email and separates observed tool output from unverified assumptions. Further investigation with genuine email evidence would be necessary to confirm a real phishing incident.
