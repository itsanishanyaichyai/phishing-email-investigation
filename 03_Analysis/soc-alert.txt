SIMULATED SOC ALERT

Alert ID: SOC-001
Case ID: PHISH-001
Alert Type: Potential Phishing Email
Severity: Medium
Status: Under Investigation
Environment: Simulated lab

Alert Description:
A fictional email claims that the recipient's Microsoft 365 account will be disabled unless the recipient verifies the account. The message contains an urgent subject line and a suspicious-looking verification URL.

Evidence:
- Fictional sender: it-support@example.invalid
- Subject: URGENT: Your Microsoft 365 Account Will Be Disabled
- URL: hxxps://microsoft365-security-check.example.invalid/verify
- Header analysis: SPF, DKIM, and DMARC failures were observed in the constructed practice header.
- URL and email-address extraction were performed using CyberChef.

Analyst Assessment:
The urgency, account-verification request, and authentication failures are suspicious indicators. However, the email and header are simulated. No real malicious activity has been confirmed.

Recommended Actions:
1. Do not click the URL or enter credentials.
2. Preserve the email and relevant evidence.
3. If this were a real incident, investigate the sender, message headers, URL reputation, and any available email-security logs.
4. Escalate for further review if supported by additional evidence.

Disposition:
Simulated phishing alert documented for portfolio practice.

Limitations:
This alert is based on fictional evidence and does not represent a confirmed real-world security incident.
