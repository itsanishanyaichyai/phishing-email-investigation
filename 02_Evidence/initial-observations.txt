PHISHING EMAIL INVESTIGATION
Initial Observations

Case ID: PHISH-001
Evidence ID: E-002

Source:
Fictional suspicious email created for cybersecurity training.

Subject:
URGENT: Your Microsoft 365 Account Will Be Disabled

Initial Observations:

1. The subject uses urgent language.
2. The email threatens account suspension within 24 hours.
3. The sender display name claims to represent IT Support.
4. The sender's identity has not been independently verified.
5. The message includes a link requesting account verification.
6. The URL uses a fictional .invalid domain.

Initial Assessment:
The message contains characteristics commonly associated with phishing, including urgency, a threat, and a verification link.

Limitations:
This is a fictional training email. These observations do not prove that a real phishing attack occurred.

Status:
Initial observations completed.
